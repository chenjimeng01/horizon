Horizon

Horizon is a lifetime cashflow and tax modelling engine for UK financial planning. It projects income, tax, wrappers and net worth year by year, and tests a plan against market variation rather than a single assumed return.

The planner itself is used by advisers and is not available here.

Starting an engagement

If you have been asked to complete the client questionnaire, it is here. It runs entirely in your browser: nothing you enter is transmitted or stored by this site. You download the completed file and send it through your own secure channel.
