What this service is, and what it is not. We prepare lifetime cashflow modelling and investment tax-drag modelling: planning illustrations built at the level of account types (pension, ISA, general account, bonds, property) and asset classes. This is not regulated financial advice. We do not recommend, review or arrange specific investments, funds or products; we do not advise on pension transfers; and nothing we produce is a personal recommendation to buy or sell any investment. That is why this form deliberately does not ask for provider names, fund names, policy or account numbers. If your goals need regulated advice, we will say so plainly and you can seek it separately.
Privacy notice (please read before completing)

Who is responsible for your data

Chenji Meng is the data controller for information you choose to send after completing this form. Contact for all data matters: the email address on your engagement letter.

What we collect and why

Names, dates of birth, contact email, and financial information at account-type level: the minimum needed to prepare the modelling you are asking for. The purpose is preparing your planning illustrations; the lawful basis is taking steps at your request to enter into a contract (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). We do not collect health information, National Insurance numbers, account numbers or identification documents through this form, and ask you not to include them in free-text answers.

How it is handled

This page stores nothing and transmits nothing. Data reaches us only when you send the generated file yourself. Once received, it is held on encrypted devices, used solely to prepare your reports, never sold or shared with third parties, and not used for automated decision-making. Fictional or anonymised versions may never be created from your data without your written agreement.

Retention and your rights

We keep engagement records for six years after the engagement ends, in line with standard financial record-keeping practice, then delete them. You may ask at any time for a copy of your data, for corrections, or for deletion (which we will honour except where retention is legally required). You may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you are unhappy with how your data is handled.
